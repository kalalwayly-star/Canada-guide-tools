Tips for Newcomers

  • Keep housing costs below 30% of your gross income whenever possible.
  • Utilities may not be included in the rent.
  • Always read the lease carefully before signing.
  • Tenant insurance is inexpensive and highly recommended.
  • Save money every month for emergencies.
